01 / Our Story

Small Roastery. Bigger Mission.

Imani is a micro-roastery in Kikuyu, Kenya.

Built from a decade in the coffee industry; brewing, roasting and sourcing coffee across different countries. We have seen the world fall for Kenyan coffee. Priced at a premium at different destinations, with value added abroad. Imani was founded on a simple conviction: Kenyan coffee branding and value addition should happen at origin.

Read about our sustainability action plan→

Through craft learned across the specialty industry, we bring art and science to modulate our roast. Roasted in small batches to iterate profiles and amplify the hard work of the producers. Each coffee bean has a character; our mission is to bring out the best from every lot we choose.

This is not a discovery story.
It is a homecoming.

03 / Sustainability

COFFEE SHOULD LEAVE MORE BEHIND.

We believe a coffee company should create value beyond the cup.

Domestic coffee consumption stands at just 3% of Kenya's production. The other 97% is exported, mostly as raw green beans, so the craft, value and story are added somewhere else.

Imani exists to change that. We roast at origin, keeping more value where the coffee grows. As Kenya works toward tripling national coffee output to 150,000 metric tonnes by 2028, our work supports a stronger local coffee economy through roasting, training and value addition.
