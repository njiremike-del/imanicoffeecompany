Brewing guide

Start with the basics

Water temperature

At Nairobi's altitude water boils at about 93 to 94°C. Pour straight off the boil for light Kenyan roasts.

Espresso

Kenyan coffee is dense and extracts slowly. Start finer and try 18 g in, 40 to 45 g out.

Coffee terms

SL28 is closely linked to Kenya's blackcurrant like acidity. AA is screen 17/18 and up, AB is screen 15 to 16. Cup quality is graded separately, and we cup every lot ourselves.
